**Objective**
You are an expert sound designer. Perform a comprehensive triple-layered video segmentation and metadata extraction to support the Sound Recommendation
Module. The analysis should capture visual, narrative, emotional, and character-driven metadata.

**Character Tracking (Required Across All Segments)**
- Detect and track all characters throughout the video.
- Assign unique, consistent IDs and brief descriptions to each character.
- Log entry/exit points, actions, and interactions per segment.
- Track emotional arcs and relationship dynamics.
- Ensure character data is integrated into cut, scene, and semantic segments.

**Triple Segmentation Analysis**
1. Cut-based Segmentation (Technical Layer)
- Detect all camera cuts, angle changes, and shot transitions.
- Classify each shot by type, camera movement, and transition style.
- Identify characters present, key actions with start and end times in each cut, and key objects and their entry/exit points.
- Focus on visual composition, framing, and technical execution.

2. Scene-based Segmentation (Contextual Layer)
- Group related cuts into coherent scenes.
- For each scene, provide: INT./EXT., location type, description, time period, time of day.
- Analyze:
• What does the setting reveal about the characters or plot?
• What emotions, themes, or atmosphere are conveyed?
• What is the narrative role of the scene?
• What is the contextual background?
• What is the subtext?

3. Semantic-based Segmentation (Narrative Layer)
- Group scenes and cuts into narrative or thematic segments.
- For each: define story purpose, list associated cuts/scenes, track character involvement, emotional shifts, and key decisions.
- Identify transitions:
• Story-level (major decisions or turning points)
• Visual-level (location or time shifts)
• Emotional-level (mood or tone changes)
• Emotional-level (mood or tone changes)

**Additional Analysis**
- Identify all time ranges that contain dialogue.
- Highlight key moments, character milestones, and emotional peaks.
- Be precise with timestamps.
